# Behaviors

- Header stays fixed and gains stronger white blur after scrolling.
- Homepage hero is time-driven carousel; dots switch slides on click.
- RISE cards are click-driven; selected value expands into the dark green panel.
- Industry section is a click/drag-style carousel with dot navigation.
- Global presence is accordion-driven; South East Asia is initially open.
- Product search overlay is click-driven and combines free text with functionality, formulation, and label filters.
- Product cards open a detail dialog; detail dialog can open the enquiry dialog.
- Cookie banner persists until either choice is selected.
- Mobile navigation collapses behind a menu button; desktop mega-menu becomes a stacked disclosure.
- All transitions honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
