# Component Inventory

- `Header`: fixed logo, primary navigation, search, language and mobile menu.
- `Hero`: image carousel, gradient, copy, pagination dots.
- `AboutSection`: split copy, award tiles and learn-more link.
- `MetricsGrid`: six photographic statistic cards.
- `RiseSection`: active explanatory panel plus R/I/S/E selectors.
- `IndustryCarousel`: three visible market cards with pagination.
- `GlobalPresence`: map, legend and region accordion.
- `NewsSection`: three article cards.
- `EsgSection`: two large photographic links.
- `ProductExperience`: search/filter overlay, result cards, detail and enquiry dialogs.
- `Footer`: four columns, contact details and legal links.
- `CookieBanner`: fixed consent prompt.
