# KLK OLEO website content

## Language

**Site**:
A distinct KLK OLEO website with its own audience, content, and editorial responsibilities. The Agrochemical site and KLK OLEO main site are separate sites.

**Content integration**:
Editorial control of visitor-facing text, images, links, cards, FAQs, navigation, and footer content through the CMS.
_Avoid_: Redesign, lead delivery integration

**Presentation**:
The approved layout, typography, responsive behavior, and animation of website content.

**Resource request**:
A visitor's request for a specific document that requires sales follow-up. It does not promise an automatic download or email delivery.
